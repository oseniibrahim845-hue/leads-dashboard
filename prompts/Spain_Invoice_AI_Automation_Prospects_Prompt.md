SPAIN SPECIFIC CONTEXT (USE FOR RESEARCH AND PERSONALIZATION)

Spanish invoicing is going through regulatory change. Use these topics when a company's website actually mentions them:

- **VeriFactu** (Reglamento de sistemas informáticos de facturación, Real Decreto 1007/2023) - invoicing software requirements from the Agencia Tributaria (AEAT)
- **Ley Crea y Crece** (Ley 18/2022) - mandatory B2B electronic invoicing
- **TicketBAI** - invoicing requirement in the Basque Country (Álava, Bizkaia, Gipuzkoa)
- **SII** (Suministro Inmediato de Información) - real time VAT reporting for larger companies
- **FACe / FACeB2B** - public sector and B2B e-invoice hubs
- **Facturae** - Spanish electronic invoice format
- **Kit Digital** - government digitalisation grants; many Spanish software vendors and consultancies are "Agentes Digitalizadores" offering invoicing and accounting solutions under it

IMPORTANT: Regulatory deadlines for VeriFactu and B2B e-invoicing have been changed more than once. Check the current dates on official sources (aeat.es, boe.es) before mentioning any date. If unsure, do not mention a date in the email.
